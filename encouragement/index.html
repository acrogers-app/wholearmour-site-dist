Week of Sunday, November 15, 2026

Chosen, Not Overlooked

Before you were anyone's son, husband, father, worker or retiree, God called you something: chosen. Peter doesn't say you might become royal or holy if you try harder. He says you are.

That matters for the man who grew up around anger, silence or absence. Your history may explain a struggle, but it doesn't get to name you. God does.

This week, read 1 Peter 2:9 out loud each morning. Mark what God has done and who you are now. Then say it as your own: "I am chosen. I belong to God." Let that truth shape one choice today: a word you hold back, a call you make, or a habit you leave behind.
